Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

Contact messages

The contact form prepares a message in your browser and opens your email app when you choose to continue. This website does not receive or store the message. If you send it, your email provider and the recipient will handle it under their own privacy policies. The prepared draft is held temporarily in this browser session until you open the email app or close the session.

Analytics

Google Analytics is not currently configured on this website. If it is enabled in the future, it will only load after you consent. Google may then process usage and device information according to Google's Privacy Policy. You can change your analytics choice below when analytics is available.

External websites

This site links to external services such as LinkedIn, WhatsApp, and Medium. Those services have their own privacy policies and data practices.
